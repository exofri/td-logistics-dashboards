Cost composition per warehouse (stacked bar, not a bar or a pie)

A plain bar would show total cost but hide the transport/storage split; a pie per warehouse would show the split but make comparing warehouses to each other hard (comparing angles across three separate pies is a known weak reading task). A stacked bar shows both at once: bar height = total cost, segment size = which lever to pull.

Highest-cost individual routes (table, not a chart)

A manager negotiating a specific route needs the exact number, not a visual estimate — same reasoning this course used for every other "exact figures a person will act on" table (e.g. the offer table in Smart Internship Match).
